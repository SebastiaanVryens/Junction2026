Transparency

Evaluation & methodology

What we test, what the numbers mean, and where the evidence is still limited.

There is no single “accuracy” score

FactsLens reports three different checks because each answers a different question:

Known-answer campaignsCan the detector surface coordinated campaigns we deliberately inserted?
Human reviewOf reviewed live clusters, how often do people agree with the flag?
Nemotron checksHow often are model conclusions grounded in the collected evidence?

A campaign test measures recall on a small synthetic set; it does not estimate precision on live traffic. Human-review ratios use only clusters people have reviewed.
