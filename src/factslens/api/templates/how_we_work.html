How We Work

What we read, how a story is compared, and what we will not do.

What we read

Every 15 minutes we read the latest headlines of Russian state media ({{ state_media|join(', ') }}), the Pravda network's own news sites ({{ pravda_sites|join(', ') }} editions) and the Telegram channels the Pravda network republishes most. Independent coverage comes from Google News, in {{ editions }} national editions.

How a story is compared

  1. Picking. NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL picks the stories that outlets in other countries also report, and skips what we compared in the last 18 hours.
  2. Searching. For each story it searches independent news in the countries concerned, in their own languages.
  3. Comparing. It sets the versions side by side: what each side says, what all sides agree on, where they contradict each other, and which claims only state media make. It also looks at the pictures and video they publish.
  4. Labelling. Each story gets one label: {{ labels.values()|join(', ')|lower }}.

Our rules

  1. Every point cites the headlines, articles or pictures it rests on, as numbered footnotes. A point that cites nothing Nemotron was shown is removed before printing.
  2. Outlets on public propaganda lists (VIGINUM's and CheckFirst's) are marked, and a claim that only they and state media make counts as “only state media”. That is weak evidence, not proof it is false.
  3. We compare what outlets say; we do not decide who is right. The sources are listed under every story: read them and judge for yourself.
  4. Pictures are shown as published by the outlet named under them.
  5. Nemotron can make mistakes. When the coverage is too thin to compare, the story says so.
  6. This edition does not follow or store anything about private people.

Where we come from

The Puppet Post began at Junction 2026 in Espoo, where it also watched swarms of accounts on Bluesky, Telegram and Mastodon. This free edition keeps the newspaper: the compared stories. The code is open at github.com/SebastiaanVryens/Junction2026.
