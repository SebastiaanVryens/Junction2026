1. Who is behind it?
The account's age, followers and public lists, or the website's registration date and owner.
2. Is a network pushing it?
We watch Bluesky, Mastodon and Telegram for groups of accounts acting together. Is this post, author, link or text among them?
3. What does independent news say?
NVIDIA Nemotron searches news in the post's language and in English, and compares outlets from different countries.
4. How does it try to persuade?
Fear appeals, loaded language, fabricated authority: each one shown with the exact words. Pictures are checked against the text.

The Lens never accuses anyone. It shows the evidence and how strong it is, and it says “not enough evidence” rather than guess. Links you check are kept in memory for six hours so the same report can be shared, then forgotten; nothing is posted anywhere.
