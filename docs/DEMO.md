# Demo video script (2:00 maximum)

Black-and-white studio intro, then a screen recording of the live site: https://srv2159312.hstgr.cloud/

## Before recording

- **Leave the reviewer edition** (masthead, top right: "Leave"). Record as a public visitor: reviewer mode shows posts and pictures of individual accounts.
- **Pre-run** the Look for the Truth link and the Ask the Truth question just before recording, and cut every wait in editing.
- **Random reviews first:** the false-positive rate on the Accuracy page shows "Not yet" until reviewers have drawn the random sample.
- Browser full screen, zoom 110–125%, no bookmarks bar. Show the address bar once.
- About 280 spoken words fit in 2 minutes: speak briskly, scroll while you talk, and keep the fanfare and spinning page to 3 seconds.
- Read figures off the screen only; they are live and change.
- Still too long? Cut Ask the Truth first, then the joke.

## Script

### 1. Studio

*Show:* the studio, the intro and the joke, then the fanfare and the front page spinning in.

*Say:* "Have you ever wondered which information can be trusted? …" (joke) "… Let us show you who's really behind the post."

### 2. Front Page

*Show:* the front page, with the address bar visible.

*Say:* "The Puppet Post runs live on Hostinger. It reads Bluesky, Telegram, Mastodon and the Pravda network's own sites. Its brain is NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL."

### 3. Contested Stories

*Show:* a story marked **Only state media report it**; scroll through Who says what and Where they contradict.

*Say:* "Every 15 minutes, Nemotron compares state media and the Pravda network with independent newspapers from 36 countries: where they contradict, and which claims only state media make."

### 4. Look for the Truth

*Show:* paste a link (e.g. the Pravda-site article from that story) and scroll through the report.

*Say:* "Paste any link. Nemotron reads the text, the pictures and the video, opens the website, checks the domain and searches independent news. It shows who is behind it, whether a swarm is pushing it, and how it persuades, like fear appeals or fabricated authority. When the evidence is thin, it says so."

### 5. Ask the Truth

*Show:* a pre-run answer.

*Say:* "Or just ask: every answer cites its sources."

### 6. The Network

*Show:* The Network, then open one group: its **Signals**, then the **Nemotron investigation**.

*Say:* "Underneath, four thousand posts a minute flow through Aiven Kafka, and Aiven PostgreSQL keeps the groups, reports and reviews. We find groups of accounts acting together and show every signal with its value and threshold. Nemotron investigates each group, and a second opinion asks: real crowd or staged? That is how breaking news stays out of our alarms."

### 7. Accuracy & Corrections

*Show:* the figures at the top of the page.

*Say:* "We measure ourselves, live: flags confirmed by reviewers, our false-positive rate on a random sample, and fake campaigns Nemotron wrote to test us. Every claim Nemotron makes must cite its evidence, and reviewers' decisions feed back into it."

### 8. Close

*Show:* the front page.

*Say:* "A person always decides. See who's really behind the post."

## What each judge hears

- **Hostinger:** live on Hostinger; groups, not single posts; signals with values and thresholds; false positives (real crowd or staged, "not enough evidence"); precision and false-positive rate.
- **NVIDIA:** Nemotron 3.5 Super VL by name; stories, Look for the Truth, Ask, investigation, the real-or-staged judge, the red team; improved by cited evidence and the reviewer feedback loop. Details: `docs/submissions/nvidia.md`.
- **Aiven:** Kafka carries every post; PostgreSQL keeps the groups, reports and reviews. Details: `docs/submissions/aiven.md`.
